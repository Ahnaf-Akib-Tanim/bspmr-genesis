Privacy policy

BSPMR collects only the information needed to manage membership, events and payments. Identity documents and contact details are visible to the secretariat only; the public directory shows name, designation, institute and photo.

Terms & conditions

Use of the member portal requires accurate information. Membership is subject to verification by the secretariat. Content published on the portal remains the responsibility of its authors.

Return & refund policy

Membership fees are non-refundable once a membership is activated. Event registration refunds, where applicable, follow the terms stated on each event and are processed to the original payment method.
