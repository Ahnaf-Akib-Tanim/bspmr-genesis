Aims & scope

A platform for rehabilitation evidence from Bangladesh

BJPMR publishes editorials, original articles, review articles and case reports across the breadth of Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation — musculoskeletal and sports medicine, neurorehabilitation, paediatric, cardiopulmonary and cancer rehabilitation, assistive technology and health-systems research — with particular attention to low-resource settings.
