Who can become a member?

Physicians working in Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation in Bangladesh, including postgraduate trainees. The secretariat verifies every application.

How long does verification take?

Applications are usually reviewed within a few working days after payment. You will be notified by SMS and email.

Can I upgrade from General to Life Membership?

Yes. Log in to the member portal and choose “Upgrade to Life Member” — you only pay the difference shown at checkout.

How do I renew a General Membership?

Your portal dashboard shows the days remaining. Renew online in one step before it expires.

Is my personal information public?

No. Only your name, designation, institute and photo appear in the public directory. Identity documents are visible to the secretariat only.
